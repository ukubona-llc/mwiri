Act IV · The question underneath the question

19:xx
The dependency risk probe

Kenneth asked about troubleshooting capacity — how many people are available when customer issues arise. This is the standard investor question dressed in operational clothing. What he was actually testing: is this a single-point-of-failure? Can it scale beyond its founder?

The exchange · verbatim recollection
Kenneth: "How many folks do you have available to attend to customer demands when troubleshooting arises?"

Abimereki: "Right now, it's me. I'll be hiring when the first customers — UG government — pay in the coming financial year."

That answer is correct. Not despite its honesty, but because of it. Anything else would have been detectable and would have undermined the credibility built by the algebra and the demos.

Analysis
What the answer actually communicates
What Kenneth heard
Pre-revenue. Founder-led. Capital-constrained. Honest about the stage. Not a liability — exactly where a seed-stage company should be. The government pipeline is the de-risking mechanism.
The embedded signal
"UG government pays → hiring unlocks." He now knows the hiring trigger. This is not a staffing problem. It is a sequencing statement. The constraint is capital, not capability.

The risk Kenneth is assessing is not "can Abimereki fix bugs?" It is "if I introduce Wayengera or Ivan, and they become early users, is there a support failure that damages my reputation?" The answer to that is handled by two things not mentioned in the room: the NDJSON append-only ledger means every issue is traceable, and the architecture is interpretable without the founder present. Those are the real answers to the troubleshooting question. They should appear in the onboarding documentation.

Near-term
Write a one-page support protocol. What happens when a user hits an error: the NDJSON trace, the GitHub audit trail, the escalation path. This document exists for Kenneth's peace of mind before any introduction is made.
FY 2026–27
Government payment → first hire. Technical support role, Uganda-based. This is the stated sequencing. Hold it.
Before pilots
SLA language in any pilot agreement. Define response time expectations upfront. A solo founder with a defined SLA is less risky than a team without one.
